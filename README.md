​1. Problem: In the modern world, continuous use of digital devices and both congenital and acquired vision impairments seriously impact people's daily lives. Constant eye strain leads to severe fatigue, weakening of visual acuity, and potential long-term health complications.
​2. Solution: To prevent these issues and enhance visual quality, the "SmartFocus Lens" project is introduced. This smart eyewear is designed for both individuals with vision impairments and those with healthy eyes who frequently work with digital screens.
​3. Technology and Working Principle: The glasses operate through two main mechanisms. First, using a physical button or slider on the frame, the user can manually adjust the lens power to suit their distance or near vision needs. Second, integrated smart sensors and an artificial intelligence system continuously monitor the condition of the eyes. When the system detects eye fatigue and determines that the current prescription is no longer suitable, it automatically suggests the most comfortable focus point or adjusts the power itself.
​4. Significance and Impact: This project not only benefits individuals with vision impairments but also protects the eye health of healthy individuals, significantly improving quality of life and preventing potential future diseases.
